Conjuring Contest

To celebrate the launch of the Icon Wizard™ 2.0, we're running the Conjuring Contest for the most creative, useful, and downright awesome icons our community can conjure. Submit your creations to the Icon Wizard Gallery between August 3, 2026 and September 7, 2026, and the Font Awesome team will pick our favorites. Winners will be announced on September 15, 2026. Win a free year of Font Awesome, plus glory and a fortune in internet points.

Who Can Enter

The Conjuring Contest is open to anyone with an active Font Awesome subscription. Folks with free accounts can build and preview compositions in the Icon Wizard, but submitting to the gallery requires an active subscription.

How to Enter

  1. Open the Icon Wizard.
  2. Stack, blend, and combine Font Awesome icons into a single custom creation.
  3. When you're happy with the result, open the Share screen.
  4. In the Conjuring Contest panel, click Submit to Gallery.

Each subscribed account can submit up to 5 icons during the contest cycle. Every submission is reviewed by the Font Awesome team before it appears in the gallery.

Categories

Winners are selected from these categories, and we may add more at our discretion.

  • Most Useful: an icon you can actually imagine using in a real project.
  • Most Creative: pure wow factor, clever layer combinations.
  • Best Remix: best transformation of existing Font Awesome icons into something new.
  • Funniest Icon: the icon that makes the team laugh out loud.

Prizes

Winners receive a free year of Font Awesome (Starter or Solo), or an account credit of equal value if you already have an active subscription, plus swag and more. All approved icons will be featured in the Icon Wizard Gallery.

Rules and Restrictions

1. Eligibility

  • You must be at least 18 years old, or 13+ with permission from a parent or legal guardian.
  • You need a Font Awesome account in good standing with an active subscription to enter.
  • Employees, contractors, and their immediate family members of Fonticons, Inc. are not eligible to win prizes.
  • The Contest is void where prohibited or restricted by law.

2. Contest Period

  • The Contest opens August 3, 2026 and closes September 7, 2026. Submissions received outside this window won't be considered.

3. Entry Requirements

  • Submissions must be built entirely within the Icon Wizard using Font Awesome icons.
  • Each submission must be your own work. Don't submit icons built by someone else.
  • You may submit up to 5 entries.

4. Originality and Content

  • Submissions must be original creations. Recognizable brand logos, trademarks, copyrighted characters, and third-party icon-set work are not eligible.
  • No offensive, discriminatory, or illegal content. We reserve the right to reject anything that doesn't fit the spirit of the gallery.
  • By entering, you represent that your submission is your original work and doesn't infringe anyone's copyright, trademark, privacy, or other rights. You agree to indemnify and hold Font Awesome harmless from any claim arising from a breach of that representation.

5. Judging and Winner Selection

  • Entries will be evaluated by the Font Awesome team on creativity, technical execution, and fit with the gallery. Judges' decisions are final.

6. Prizes

  • Winners receive a free year of Font Awesome (Starter or Solo), or an account credit of equal value if you already have an active subscription.
  • One prize per person across all categories.
  • Prizes are non-transferable. No cash substitution, though we may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if necessary.
  • Winners are responsible for any taxes that apply to their prize.
  • Winners under 18 must have a parent or legal guardian accept the prize on their behalf.

7. Moderation

  • Every submission is reviewed by the Font Awesome team before it appears in the gallery. During the Contest, we aim to review entries within 3 business days. We may remove any submission at our discretion if it violates these guidelines.

You waive any claim against Font Awesome arising from our creation, use, or distribution of such icons.

9. Your Information

  • Providing a social handle is optional. If you include one, we'll display it alongside your creation to credit you when it appears in the gallery or in Font Awesome marketing, for as long as your creation is featured.
  • If you win, we'll email the address on your Font Awesome account to notify you.

What's By Icon selection?

Instead of including thousands of Font Awesome icons in your Kit, you hand-pick just the icons you need for your project to get the lightest, speediest performance.
